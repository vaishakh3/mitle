# Play declarations and content-rating notes

- Target audience: adults 18 and older only.
- Dating/relationship functionality: yes.
- User-to-user communication: no free-form chat; only predefined day-of statuses and a mutually unlocked post-meet note.
- User-generated content: private spot hint, support text, safety reports, and optional mutual note.
- Location: approximate foreground location only; no background location.
- Violence, gambling, drugs, financial, health, government, news, and ads functionality: none.
- Emergency guidance: links to India’s 112 service; Milte clearly states it is not an emergency service.
- Account creation: passwordless email OTP.
- Account deletion: available inside the app.
- App access for review: a dedicated controlled reviewer account exists and reusable sign-in instructions are saved in Play Console. No inbox or OTP access is required by the reviewer.
