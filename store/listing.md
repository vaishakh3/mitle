## Full description

Milte is the dating app designed to get you off dating apps.

There are no photos to optimise, profiles to perform, swipe decks, follower counts, or chats that stretch for weeks. Milte privately considers mutual boundaries, real availability, distance, a few interests, and the kind of first hour each person is comfortable with.

If there is a fit, one anonymous possibility appears. You both decide independently. Saying no is private. Only after two yeses does Milte reveal a named public meeting place, a one-hour window, and each person’s simple “how to spot me” clue.

Before you leave:

- both people privately confirm the plan still works;
- the meeting place comes from current map data and is visible in Maps;
- a shared recognition phrase helps you identify the right person;
- share-plan, calendar, and safety actions remain one tap away.

On the day, status-only updates—on my way, arrived, running late, or can’t make it—remove uncertainty without turning into chat. A confidential report ends the plan and prevents the same pair from matching again.

When the hour ends, the live match disappears. If you both say you met and independently leave a Second Chapter note, the notes are revealed once. Otherwise, the app lets the moment go.

Milte is for adults aged 18 and older. It is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee another person’s identity, behaviour, attendance, or compatibility. Meet in public, arrange your own travel, share your plan, trust your judgment, and leave whenever you want. In an emergency in India, call 112.

No feed. Just show up.
