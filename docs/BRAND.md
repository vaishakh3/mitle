# Milte brand system

## Brand idea

**Meet for real.**

Milte is an India-born dating product built around one person, one public place, and one real hour. It should feel grown-up, direct, private, warm, and quietly bold. Romance comes from anticipation and restraint—not hearts, destiny claims, streaks, swipes, or synthetic compatibility theatre.

## Identity

The primary mark is the exact lowercase wordmark **milte?**. It reads first as the name and then as the Hinglish invitation behind it: shall we meet? Archivo gives it a direct, contemporary silhouette; the signal-red question mark is the ownable gesture. The compact **m?** lettermark is used only when the full name cannot fit, such as the launcher icon and favicon. It uses a white `m` and marigold question mark on ultramarine, with enough clear space to survive every Android icon mask.

The chair-and-chai and crossed-path screen prints are supporting editorial illustrations, never logos. This separation keeps the identity instantly legible while preserving the warmth and personality of the product artwork.

Eight soft geometric member avatars extend the identity without using photos.
Their deliberately abstract faces lower comparison pressure while making the
product feel inhabited. Each account also receives one unique, immutable random
username. The meeting ticket is the most expressive UI application: a crisp
ultramarine place panel and red/marigold meeting-point geometry, never blurred
raster art behind the date details.

| Leaf | `#25734B` | Confirmed and arrived states |

DM Sans is the functional voice. Archivo is the direct, roman display voice. The product uses a cool light canvas, flat colour, compact geometry, precise spacing, and occasional screen-print illustration. Primary actions use ultramarine; red is reserved for connection or urgency; marigold identifies the shared place. Shadows are reserved for modal layers, while the meeting ticket uses a defined paper edge. It does not use gradients, italic-serif heroes, neon glow, glass surfaces, floating blobs, generic rounded-card stacks, stock-romance imagery, or ornamental Indian stereotypes. See `DESIGN.md` for the enforceable system and anti-references.

## Voice

Preferred lines:

- “milte?”
- “meet for real.”
- “two private yeses.”
- “no feed. just show up.”
- “saying no is private.”

Safety and operations copy is always literal. Never obscure a risk, cancellation, report consequence, retention rule, or emergency action behind poetic language.

## Source of truth

The current production compact-mark source is [`brand/milte-question.svg`](../brand/milte-question.svg). The previous bracket identity under `brand/milte/` is retained only as design history. Final store screenshots must come from the signed application.
