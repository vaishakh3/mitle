# Milte brand system

## Brand idea

**Meet for real.**

Milte is an India-born dating product built around one person, one public place, and one real hour. It should feel grown-up, direct, private, warm, and quietly bold. Romance comes from anticipation and restraint—not hearts, destiny claims, streaks, swipes, or synthetic compatibility theatre.

## Identity

The mark is two independent architectural brackets surrounding one meeting point. The people remain separate; the shared hour sits safely between them. It is intentionally not a heart, chat bubble, location pin, human silhouette, or letter monogram.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Midnight Ink | `#0B0A0D` | Primary canvas and type |
| Warm Paper | `#F4EDE3` | Reversed type and physical-feeling surfaces |
| Gulmohar | `#A73550` | Mark, meeting point, and primary actions |
| Accessible Gulmohar text | `#D7A2AD` | Small accent text on Midnight Ink |
| Marigold | `#E7A45A` | Time-sensitive states |
| Rain Blue | `#55758C` | Calm utility and safety |
| Leaf | `#7E9471` | Confirmed and arrived states |

DM Sans is the functional voice. Fraunces is limited to short emotional moments. The product uses flat color, visible keylines, precise spacing, and occasional cropped bracket geometry. It does not use gradients, neon glow, glass surfaces, floating blobs, generic rounded-card stacks, stock-romance imagery, or ornamental Indian stereotypes.

## Voice

Preferred lines:

- “milte?”
- “meet for real.”
- “two private yeses.”
- “no feed. just show up.”
- “saying no is private.”

Safety and operations copy is always literal. Never obscure a risk, cancellation, report consequence, retention rule, or emergency action behind poetic language.
