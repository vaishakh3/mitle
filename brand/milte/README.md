# Milte identity system

Milte (pronounced **mill-tay**) is an India-born dating product that helps two people meet in real life without turning either person into content.

## Brand idea

**Meet for real.**

One person. One public place. One hour. No browsing, profiles, chat, follower counts, or feed. The identity is built around a protected meeting point: two independent sides remain visibly separate around one shared moment.

## Personality

- Grown-up, direct, warm, and quietly bold.
- Modern Indian without ornamental stereotypes.
- Romantic through anticipation and restraint, never destiny language.
- Safety is plain-spoken and visible, never hidden behind poetic copy.

## Mark

The mark contains two architectural brackets and one circle:

- the brackets are two independent people and an open public threshold;
- the negative space protects privacy before the meeting;
- the gulmohar circle is one mutually chosen hour.

Do not redraw the proportions, move the circle, close the mark into a badge, or turn it into people, a heart, a bow tie, or a letter H. Minimum digital size is 24 px. Clear space is the diameter of the center circle on every side.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Midnight Ink | `#0B0A0D` | Primary dark canvas and type |
| Warm Paper | `#F4EDE3` | Primary light canvas and reversed mark |
| Gulmohar | `#A73550` | Decision, meeting point, primary action |
| Marigold | `#E7A45A` | Recognition phrase and time-sensitive detail |
| Rain Blue | `#55758C` | Safety and calm utility states |
| Leaf | `#7E9471` | Confirmed and arrived states |

Gulmohar is an accent, not a full-page default. Do not add gradients or neon variants.

## Type

- **DM Sans** is the product and wordmark-adjacent voice: navigation, controls, forms, body copy, and campaign statements.
- **Fraunces** is reserved for one-line emotional moments such as “tomorrow has a place.” It should not be used for long instructions, safety copy, or dense settings.
- Lowercase is the default campaign voice. Sentence case is used for safety, legal, and operational clarity.

## Voice

Use short, specific lines:

- “milte?”
- “meet for real.”
- “two private yesses.”
- “no feed. just show up.”
- “saying no is private.”

Avoid compatibility scores, destiny claims, gamification, urgency pressure, generic enthusiasm, and euphemistic safety language.

## Photography

Use documentary-style public India at blue hour: metro plazas, parks, museums, bookshops, promenades, and cultural districts. Subjects should be naturally present in a public environment; avoid staged affection, identifiable faces, luxury-cafe clichés, bokeh romance, and people looking at camera.

## Asset status

- `vector/` contains the clean production geometry.
- `exports/` contains raster icon exports rendered from the SVG sources.
- `generated/` contains accepted ImageGen identity-direction boards.
- `mockups/` contains product and campaign concepts; they are not literal production screenshots.
- `working/` contains rejected explorations retained for design history.
- `PROMPTS.md` records every ImageGen prompt used in built-in generation mode.

The generated Play feature graphic is a direction reference only because environmental signage is synthetic. Build the final Play asset from the vector system and verified photography. The product UI board is a design target, not a substitute for screenshots from the actual app.
